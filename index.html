The idea in brief

Estate planning may look like paperwork. It is also a chance to decide how the gifts God entrusted to us will keep serving our families, our neighbors, and His Church after we are gone.

The Final Gift helps you see that planning as an expression of faith, gratitude, and hope:

  1. Item IA way to care for those you love.
  2. Item IIA way to express the values that guide your life.
  3. Item IIIA way to support the ministries and causes closest to your heart.
