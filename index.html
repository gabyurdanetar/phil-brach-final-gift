Estate planning may look like paperwork. It is also a chance to decide how the gifts God entrusted to us will keep serving our families, our neighbors, and His Church after we are gone.

The Final Gift helps you see that planning as an expression of faith, gratitude, and hope: a way to care for those you love, express the values that guide your life, and support the ministries and causes closest to your heart.

The Final Gift

A Spirituality of Estate Gift Planning

This is not a how-to guide to writing an estate plan. It is a short, accessible book about why planning matters, written for families, parishes, and the people who serve them. Read it in one sitting, discuss it with a group, or keep it beside you as you work with an attorney and financial adviser.
